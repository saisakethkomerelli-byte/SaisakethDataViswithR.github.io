The goal of this exercise was to turn a bivariate data visualisation into a multivariate data visualisation. I searched for a bivariate data visualisation that included other variables in its data that i could use. I found this visualsation which comparing the overall human rights index every year. The data included individual countries so I filtered down to 6 countries and added that as my third variable in the visualisation. Thus it became a multivariate data visualisation.
