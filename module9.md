Summary of work:
The goal of this exercise was to make an interactive data visualisation. I decided to add on to my previous data visualisation that I created which was a choropleth that represented the human rights index values of every country in the year 2025.
I added interactivity to this data visualisation by adding a hover feature. When I hover above a country a card pops out showcasing the information about the country's name, continent and human index value.
